Contact

FracSTAT is developed and maintained by a small academic team spanning several institutions. The best way to reach us depends on what you need — see the options below.

1. Bug Reports and Feature Requests

For anything code-related — a bug, an unexpected calculation result, a crash, or a feature request for a future release — the fastest and most trackable route is to open an issue directly on the GitHub repository. Please include your operating system, the FracSTAT version, and, where possible, the steps needed to reproduce the issue (and a sample dataset, if the issue is data-specific and shareable).
